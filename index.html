🤖 Artificial Intelligence Policy in Sacred Gospel Study

BYU-Pathway Academic Honesty Policy & The CES Honor Code

Honor Code Aligned
⚖️ Official Classification

BYU-Pathway Worldwide's Academic Honesty Policy explicitly classifies the "misuse of generative or artificial intelligence" as academic misconduct (alongside plagiarism and cheating). In REL 250 B, our sacred focus is your authentic relationship with the Lord Jesus Christ.

Permissible Study Aid (When Syllabus Permits)
  • Scripture Cross-Referencing: Searching for parallel scriptures on the Bread of Life, the Atonement, or the Resurrection.
  • Historical & Cultural Context: Looking up ancient customs, geography of Galilee/Jerusalem, or Hebrew/Greek word roots.
  • Study Outlining: Organizing study points before writing your own reflection.
Prohibited Misuse (Honor Code Breach)
  • Automated Spiritual Testimony: Using AI to write your personal testimony, spiritual ponderings, or scripture journals.
  • Canvas Discussion Automation: Copying and pasting AI-generated text into weekly class discussion boards.
  • Bypassing Scripture Reading: Relying on AI summaries instead of reading the word of God directly.

📖 Purpose of Course Assignments

The assignments in REL 250 B are designed to assess each student's individual learning, personal perspective, and direct study of the assigned scriptures. Please ensure all reflections and discussion posts reflect your own authentic work.
